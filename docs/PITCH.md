# SpeakBuddy | One-page pitch

**Natural, supportive English-speaking practice for the conversation that matters next.**

A placement candidate can understand every word of an interview question and still struggle to answer it out loud. One classroom period gives each student little private speaking time. A student who thinks partly in Kannada, Hindi or Tamil often needs a few extra seconds before they can finish a thought in English.

SpeakBuddy gives that student a five-minute rehearsal with a front-facing fictional AI tutor. The learner chooses a situation, speaks or types, checks what was heard and decides when to send it. The tutor models one useful correction and poses a related question. At the end, the learner leaves with the phrase they used, a clearer alternative and one practice task. Saved reports make the next rehearsal more specific.

**Show it in ninety seconds.** Choose Job interview and a Kannada bridge. Say “Yesterday I go to college to meet my team.” Review the transcript and send. The tutor models “Yesterday I went to college to meet my team” and asks what the learner contributed. Open the report, choose a confidence rating and revisit the pattern in Progress.

**First buyer and business model.** Recruit one college placement cell for a four-week, opt-in adult pilot. Explore a per-active-learner semester licence with scenario assignments and aggregate cohort outcomes. Test a ₹149/month individual plan and a ₹199/active learner/semester institutional offer as interview hypotheses. Validate willingness to pay, tax and support costs before committing to a price.

**What exists.** A runnable iPhone-framed web app, two original tutors, seven role-plays, multilingual starters, speech controls, a correction overlay, opt-in memory and reports. Guided practice needs no paid key. A configured live mode adds transcription and context-aware AI replies. The current avatar uses expression frames; phoneme-level lip-sync and assessed pronunciation belong to the next pilot.

**Economics and evidence.** The reference live-text/STT mix costs about $12.15 in variable AI charges per 1,000 five-minute sessions, before hosting and tax. Pilot measures include successful three-turn conversations, seven-day return, independently rated task transfer and teacher-reviewed correction precision. All projections and pilot goals are unvalidated. [Cost assumptions and primary sources](PRODUCT.md#cost-model)

**The ask.** One placement coordinator, one English faculty reviewer and 30 adult volunteers for four weeks. Give learners two comparable interview prompts, have the teacher rate them blind, and use the results to decide whether to fund real-time voice and richer avatar rendering.

## Eight-slide presentation outline

| Slide | Story to tell | Evidence or visual |
|---|---|---|
| 1. The opening question | “Tell me about yourself.” Tell the story of a learner who needs more time to answer. | One short fictional exchange, identified as an example. |
| 2. First audience | Adult Indian college students in placement preparation and the coordinator trying to serve them. | Cohort recruitment plan and three user interviews when available. |
| 3. Product demo | Run the ninety-second demo above in the app. | Actual call, correction and report. |
| 4. Teaching method | Confirmed turns, one helpful recast, a follow-up and a next-session task. | Before/after sentence with the same intended meaning. |
| 5. How it works | Separate voice capture, conversation, rendering and ground-truth feedback. | Architecture diagram and one latency measurement when available. |
| 6. Why a college buys | Private repetitions between classes and low marginal delivery cost. | Cost per completed practice and two price hypotheses. |
| 7. Evidence and trust | Measure independent transfer and retention. Explain learner consent and the 18+ pilot. | Rubric, sample size and safeguards; clearly label targets. |
| 8. Team, milestones and ask | Show weeks 4/8/12 and ask for a college partner and teacher reviewer. | Named roles, pilot duration and decision gate. |

For teachers, spend more time on correction accuracy and the independent task rubric. For investors, show real retention and margin after the pilot. For hackathon judges, use the working demo and show the fallback by declining the microphone.
